Here's where most people mess up. They get 300 people that might be interested… then send a 3000 word pitch. This is how we close deals :

Hey [Name], Quick question - what's your biggest challenge with [topic] right now?

​

That's it. 23 words. No pitch. Just value and curiosity.

80% will answer your question. Now you're in a conversation.

Interesting - we just solved that exact problem for [similar company]. Mind if I send you a 3-minute video showing how?

​

27 words. Still no hard pitch. Just relevance and value.

When they say yes (and 60% do), record a Loom showing:

Acknowledgment of their specific challenge

Quick case study of similar client

Your unique approach

Soft CTA to discuss their situation

Hey [Name], did you get a chance to check my message above? Noticed you're [specific observation about their business] - we should chat.

​

29 words. Personal. Relevant. Direct.

Let's be real - managing 500 conversations is impossible without AI. But most AI messages sound robotic. Here are the prompts we use to sound human:

Look at this person's LinkedIn profile: [profile URL] Identify: 1. Their biggest business challenge based on their recent posts 2. Something specific they're working on right now 3. A genuine compliment about their work Keep it under 50 words total.
