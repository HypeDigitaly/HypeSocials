## 0. Read this before applying anything

### 0.1 Hard constraint discovered in the parser (binding on T1.6)

`styles.py:172-207` builds `MetaStyle` with explicit `item.get(...)` calls. **An unrecognised top-level key in a style
entry is silently ignored — it does not error, and it never reaches the render prompt.** The same is true of
`LayoutZone`, which reads exactly `position`, `content`, `text_treatment`, `role`.

Therefore this report proposes **no new fields** except `list_mode` (which T1.5 is adding to the parser). Everything
else — type scale, palette roles, grid/margins, motif inventory, deck pacing, list treatment prose — must be written
**into the existing fields**:

| What is missing | Where it must go |
|---|---|
| type scale & hierarchy ratios | `typography` |
| colour ROLES + coverage limits | `palette` (each entry is a free string — put the role in the string) |
| grid, margins, safe area, alignment axis | `text_placement` |
| motif inventory, texture rules, motif budget | `image_treatment` |
| deck rhythm, what may/must-not change slide to slide | `visual_pacing` + `per_format_guidance.carousel_slide` |
| per-zone type spec | `layout_zones[].text_treatment` |
| list/table treatment | **`list_mode`** (new, spec §4b) |

Second constraint: `render_prompt` is capped at ~120 words and pre-flight warns above it. **Do not grow
`render_prompt` to carry any of this.** The DNA fields are unbounded and are what `style_dna` ships byte-identically
across a deck — they are the correct home.

Third constraint: `carousel_role` lives *inside* `per_format_guidance` (`styles.py:322` reads
`per_format_guidance.get("carousel_role")`). Three styles already place it there correctly; keep it there.

### 0.2 Effective text budgets (for every "budget realism" verdict below)

Config ships `image_headline: 90`, `image_subline: 160`, `slide: 300`, `reel_seed_headline: 60`. Effective ceiling is
`min(config, style cap)`. A style cap **above** the config number is dead text — it can never bind. A style cap
**below** it is the real limit, and on a panel-mapped deck (FR-304) a panel over the limit leaves that slide
**wordless**. So a too-low cap is not a shorter slide, it is an **empty slide** — this is the single highest-leverage
thing in this file.

## 1. `photoreal-ambient-caption`

**Sources:** `Informative_Photorealistic_Carousel_01/02/03.webp`
(01 = dark night kitchen, centred 6-line white sans; 02 = bright daylight loft, upper-left 2-line bold white sans;
03 = near-black night office over a city window, one centred line.)

### Gaps
1. **Type scale absent.** `typography` names a family species and a weight range but no size relationships. There is
   no cap height as a fraction of frame, no minimum legible size, no line-length target. The model therefore re-picks
   the caption size on every slide and the deck's rhythm breaks.
2. **Weight contradicts the source.** Style says *"regular-to-semibold"*. All three inspiration frames are **bold /
   heavy** — 02 in particular is a heavy grotesque. Regular white text over a photograph at feed size is the classic
   unreadable outcome; the source knows this and the YAML does not.
3. **Light direction over-specified to one option.** `render_prompt` mandates *"bright diffuse window daylight on pale
   walls"*. Two of the three sources are **night interiors** (01 kitchen at night, 03 black night office). The style as
   written cannot produce two-thirds of its own reference material. Either widen it to two named, resolved lighting
   states (and pick one per deck, held for the whole deck), or accept that the night look is unreachable.
4. **Text position over-specified to one option, and it disagrees with the source.** Style mandates *upper-left third*;
   sources 01 and 03 centre the block on the optical middle. Recommend: keep upper-left as the **deck default**, and
   sanction a centred variant *chosen once per deck* (never per slide).
5. **Plate rule stated twice, differently.** `layout_zones[0].text_treatment` says "white with a faint drop shadow, up
   to five tight lines" and never mentions the plate; `text_placement` says short strings float and longer strings sit
   on a "soft charcoal plate at roughly a quarter opacity". Two fields, two contracts, no threshold. State the trigger
   once, numerically, in both places.
6. **No contrast floor.** This is the only defence a white-on-photo style has. Nothing tells the model what to do when
   the chosen region is bright (source 02's ceiling is near-white and the text survives only because it is heavy).
7. **Palette has no roles.** Five hexes, no statement of which is ground, which is the accent object, which is text.
   The "one warm accent object" in `render_prompt` is never tied to `pale oak #C2A278`.
8. **No margins / safe area.** "clear of every edge" is unmeasurable, and a 4:5 crop of a 3:4 render will eat the top.
9. **No deck-pacing contract.** `carousel_slide` says "move closer into the SAME space" but never says what must stay
   pinned (camera height, light direction, palette, caption x-position) versus what may change.
10. **No list treatment.** A `slide: 240` panel arriving as "1. … 2. … 3. …" has nowhere to go — it will be set as one
    prose blob on the plate. Needs `list_mode`.
11. **Faces.** Source 02 shows an identifiable face; the style forbids it. **Keep the ban** — it is a deliberate
    §0.12/leakage-safety divergence, not a defect. Worth one comment line in the YAML so a future author doesn't
    "fix" it back.

### Budget realism — **PASS with one caution**
`headline 90 / subline 120 / slide 240 / overlay 60`. Five tight lines at ~48 chars/line ≈ 240 — the declared 240 is
exactly what the described block holds, which is good. Caution: `subline 120` is below config's 160 and this style has
**no subline zone at all** (one zone only), so the number is inert-but-misleading; either add a subline zone or set it
equal to the headline cap so the style reads honestly.

### SUGGESTED FIELD TEXT

```yaml
    typography: >-
      One neutral grotesque sans only (Inter, Helvetica Now, or the same species) — no second family, no display
      face, no script, no all-caps. Caption sets SEMIBOLD to BOLD (never lighter: this type sits on a photograph and
      a light weight disappears at feed size). Cap height is 4.5-6% of the frame height on every slide of a deck —
      the same size on all of them, never re-scaled per slide. Line length 26-42 characters; leading 1.12-1.20x the
      cap height; sentence case, normal tracking, no outline, no letterspacing.
    palette:
      - "GROUND — off-white plaster #EDEAE4: the walls and the majority surface of every frame"
      - "SURFACE — warm grey #B7B2AA: soft furnishing, floor and shadow-side of the ground"
      - "ACCENT OBJECT — pale oak #C2A278: exactly one warm wooden or ceramic object per frame, never two"
      - "SHADOW — near-black #14120F: cast shadow, doorway, and the caption plate at low opacity"
      - "TEXT — caption white #FFFFFF: the caption block and nothing else in the frame is this bright"
      - "Accent and shadow together stay under a quarter of the frame; the ground carries the rest"
    text_placement: >-
      One caption block, upper-left third, its left edge on a margin of 8% of the frame width and its top edge at 10%
      of the frame height; nothing text-bearing enters the outer 8% on any side, and the bottom 12% stays clear so a
      4:5 crop never touches the words. The block sits over low-detail negative space, never across the subject and
      never across a hard edge. Under 60 characters it floats with a faint drop shadow; at 60 characters or more, and
      always when the region behind it is lighter than mid-grey, it sits on a soft charcoal plate at roughly a quarter
      opacity with rounded corners and inner padding of about one cap height on every side. The caption keeps the same
      x-position, the same size and the same treatment on every slide of one deck.
    visual_pacing: >-
      One idea per frame: the room does the talking and the caption only names it — one text block, one focal object,
      no second competing subject. Across a deck the camera height, the light direction, the palette and the caption's
      position and size are FIXED; only the crop distance and the one accent object change. Cover is the widest view
      of the space; each body slide steps closer into that same space, never into a new one.
    list_mode:
      reflow_over_chars: 120
      max_rows: 5
      layout: >-
        A list panel sets as one left-aligned column on the charcoal plate: every source line keeps its own row,
        a leading number or bullet stays welded to its own row's text, rows share one left edge and one type size,
        and an over-long row wraps under its own first word with a hanging indent. Rows shrink together, never one
        alone; the plate grows downward to hold them and the photograph behind it simplifies to accommodate it.
      overflow: reflow
```

## 2. `editorial-voxel-carousel`

**Sources:** `Informative_Carousel_01.png` (cover: white ground, orange kicker, black Didone headline, orange + green
payoff words, hand-drawn double rule, two voxel mascots, orange sparkles, `01 / 10` chip, handwritten `swipe→`),
`Informative_Carousel_03.png` (cream ground, blue Didone display second line, blue double rule, handwritten script
sub-line, four white rounded icon+label cards with REAL tool logos, a dark terminal card with green + blue mono text,
a glowing 3D hero, blue sparkles).

### Gaps & contradictions
1. **Accent colour contradicts both sources.** YAML declares exactly one accent, `teal #0C8897`. Source 01 uses
   **orange (#E8501E-ish) as the primary accent plus green (#1E8F63-ish) as a second contrast accent**; source 03 uses
   **blue (#2F6FE0-ish)** with an orange hero and a yellow sparkle. Two decisions are needed and neither is in the file:
   (a) is the accent per-registry-fixed or per-deck-chosen? (b) is there **one** accent or an accent **pair**? The
   source grammar is clearly "one accent + one contrast accent for the second half of a two-part payoff" — that is how
   `Claude Code + Codex` reads at all. As written, a two-part payoff has no way to differentiate its halves.
2. **Lettered swipe cue contradicts the source.** Exclusion: *"lettered navigation cues — the top-right swipe mark is
   a drawn arrow glyph and carries no words"*. **Both** sources letter it (`swipe→` handwritten in 01; `SWIPE →` in the
   letterpress siblings). The ban is defensible on leakage grounds (an invented word is invented text), but then say so
   — and the zone should describe the arrow's drawn character precisely so it doesn't come back as a random chevron.
3. **Terminal card forbids the thing the source does.** Exclusion: *"legible text inside a screenshot, terminal card…
   greeked into grey bars unless quoted in the TEXT block"*. Source 03's terminal card is the single best element on
   the slide and it is **fully legible** (`> speak / > route / > remember / > RUNS YOUR WHOLE DAY_`). Under the current
   rule that card renders as grey bars and the slide loses its punchline. This needs the same greeking amendment
   flagged for `platform-showcase-card` (§7.2): greeked bars are **background mock-up filler**, and any planned
   verbatim string routed into the card renders literally.
4. **Icon-and-label list is described but never specified.** `layout_zones[2]` says "icon-and-label rows in medium
   sans" — no row count, no row height, no card treatment, no label/value hierarchy. Source 03 is precise: **white
   rounded-square icon tile, bold uppercase label, grey regular sub-label, four rows, generous gutter**. This is the
   style's most useful asset and it is the least specified thing in the entry.
5. **No type scale.** Three families are named (Didone, grotesque, script) with zero size relationship between them.
   In the source the ratio is roughly kicker : headline : body = 1 : 4 : 1.4, and the headline's second line is set
   LARGER than the first. None of that is recorded.
6. **Hero object rules incomplete.** "one physically rendered voxel toy figure" — source 01 has **two** figures
   interacting; source 03 has a hero plus a prop. The count rule is wrong and the "interaction" idea (the two figures
   collaborating over a diagram) is the entire concept of frame 01.
7. **Sparkle / annotation motif has no budget.** "four-point sparkles, motion ticks, one script annotation with an
   arrow" — no count, no placement rule, no size. Sources use 4-8 sparkles clustered around the hero only.
8. **Deck pacing under-specified.** `carousel_slide` says drop the headline "by roughly a third" — good — but nothing
   pins the ground colour (01 is white, 03 is cream: within one deck that would read as two decks).
9. **No `list_mode`.** This is the most list-hungry style in the registry (`text_density: high`, icon rows, terminal
   card) and it has no list treatment at all.
10. **Counter chip conditional is right, but its form is unspecified.** Source: `01 / 10` — the position number is
    heavy black, the total is lighter grey, inside a black-outlined rounded rect. Worth pinning, it reads as a system.

### Budget realism — **FAIL (caps are dead numbers)**
`headline 110 / subline 160 / slide 300`. Headline 110 is above config's 90, so it never binds — the effective
headline cap is 90. But the described headline is "very large, two to three lines" of a Didone: 90 characters at that
size does not fit a 4:5 frame at the declared scale. Either the headline zone must be allowed to step down a scale
tier as characters grow (say so explicitly), or the practical cap is ~60. `slide: 300` is honest **only if** the style
states where 300 characters set — currently there is no zone that can hold them except the "one annotation line".

### SUGGESTED FIELD TEXT

```yaml
    typography: >-
      Three families, never a fourth. (1) High-contrast Didone serif for the headline: hairline-to-heavy stroke
      contrast, tight leading at 0.92-1.00 of cap height, set in two or three lines with the payoff line set LARGER
      than the setup line. (2) Tracked-out uppercase grotesque sans, bold, for the kicker, the position chip and every
      list label — tracking +8%, cap height one quarter of the headline's. (3) One handwritten script, reserved for a
      single annotation line, never for a headline and never for a label. Scale relationship, held on every slide:
      kicker 1x, list label 1.3x, body 1.4x, headline 4x. Nothing on the frame sets between the body size and the
      headline size — the jump is the style.
    palette:
      - "GROUND — warm cream #F6F0E4: flat, unbroken, fills the frame edge to edge on every slide of a deck"
      - "PRIMARY TEXT — near-black #14120F: kicker, headline setup lines, list labels, chip outline"
      - "ACCENT — saturated teal #0C8897: the payoff phrase, the double rule, sparkles, ticks, the script annotation
         and the drawn arrow; never more than one tenth of the frame"
      - "CONTRAST ACCENT — burnt orange #E8501E: used ONCE per frame at most, and only to separate the second half of
         a two-part payoff phrase from the first; absent otherwise"
      - "DEPTH — paper shadow grey #CFC7B8: the hero object's contact shadow and nothing else"
      - "SURFACE — white card #FFFFFF: list tiles and the terminal card's border; the only pure white in the frame"
    text_placement: >-
      A four-band vertical grid on a 6% outer margin. Band 1 (top 10%): position chip flush left, drawn swipe arrow
      flush right. Band 2 (10-45%): kicker, then the headline, then the teal double rule closing it — all flush left
      to the margin, ragged right. Band 3 (45-62%): the quoted string as short left-aligned lines, or the icon-and-label
      column, or the terminal card. Band 4 (62-100%): the hero object, centred on the frame's vertical axis, its
      contact shadow inside the bottom margin. Band boundaries are identical on every slide of one deck; only the
      content of band 3 changes.
    image_treatment: >-
      Physically rendered voxel and glossy 3D props photographed as studio product shots — matte moulded surfaces, soft
      contact shadows, gentle specular highlights — floating on the flat cream field with no environment behind them.
      One or two hero objects: two only when they are DOING something together (working on a shared prop), never two
      objects merely placed side by side. Hand-drawn ink accents in the accent colour, budgeted: four to eight
      four-point sparkles clustered around the hero, up to three short motion ticks, exactly one script annotation with
      one drawn arrow, and the one double rule under the headline. No photography of real places, no gradient
      background, no drop shadow under flat elements, no glow.
    visual_pacing: >-
      Loud headline, quiet ground, one hero. Every slide repeats the same three-part rhythm — kicker, headline,
      object — so the deck reads as one magazine. Across a deck the ground colour, the accent colour, the margin, the
      band boundaries, the rule weight and the chip form are FIXED; only the headline's line count, the band-3 content
      and the hero object change. A deck never mixes a white ground with a cream one.
    list_mode:
      reflow_over_chars: 160
      max_rows: 6
      layout: >-
        A list panel sets as an icon-and-label column in band 3: one white rounded-square tile per row carrying an
        abstract glyph (or the real mark when the TOOL MARKS line sanctions it), then the row's label in tracked-out
        uppercase bold sans, then its value or description directly under the label in regular sans at 0.75x the label
        size, in shadow grey. Every source line is one row; a label and its value never separate, never swap rows and
        never merge with a neighbouring row. Rows share one left edge, one tile size and one baseline rhythm; all rows
        shrink together to fit, never one alone; an over-long row wraps under its own label with a hanging indent.
        Beyond six rows the column splits into two equal columns filled top-to-bottom, left column first.
      overflow: two_column
```

Also amend the terminal/screenshot exclusion to the greeking rule in §7.2 and note the swipe-word ban as deliberate.

## 3. `letterpress-print-carousel`

**Sources:** `Informative_Carousel_05.png` (cover: aged cream paper, orange tracked kicker, huge condensed black
woodtype over a vermilion payoff line, hand-drawn underline, small lowercase deck copy, hand-circled `SWIPE >>`,
pixel-art mascot, `01 / 10` chip in vermilion outline), `Informative_Carousel_04.png` (body: terracotta ground, mono
meta line top-left, black label chip `PLUGIN 02 / IMPECCABLE`, cream ultra-heavy lowercase headline with two words
reversed in black, cream body paragraph with a bold phrase, a taped white card holding three legible UI screenshots,
`03 / 07` bottom-left, `SWIPE →` bottom-right).

### Gaps & contradictions
1. **Two grounds, no rule for which.** Cover = aged cream; body = saturated terracotta. `per_format_guidance` records
   this correctly (good — this is the best-specified divergence in the registry). But the palette lists both without
   saying they are *alternating* rather than *mixable*, and nothing forbids a cream body slide mid-deck.
2. **Ink-count rule is broken by the style's own palette.** `render_prompt` says *"one paper tone plus exactly two ink
   colours"*. The palette lists five entries and the cover uses black + vermilion + orange kicker on cream = three inks
   on one paper. Resolve: name the two inks per ground state.
3. **Lettered swipe cue contradicts both sources** (same as §2.2) — sources letter it in two different ways
   (`SWIPE >>` hand-circled on the cover; `SWIPE →` mono, bottom-right, on the body). Ban is defensible; make it
   explicit and describe the drawn mark's character.
4. **Screenshot inset rule contradicts the source and destroys the source's best element.** Exclusion says taped
   screenshot cards are "invented and either wordless or greeked". Source 04's taped card carries three legible
   mockups whose headings are readable — that legibility is what makes the slide credible. Same greeking amendment as
   §7.2.
5. **Mono meta line is undeclared.** Source 04 carries `JUN ©2026 · CHASE AI · 03 / 07` top-left in letterspaced
   mono — a real, repeating, system-level device with no zone in the YAML. Either add it as a zone (conditional on a
   quoted string, like the counter) or state that it is deliberately dropped.
6. **The black label chip is undeclared.** Source 04's `PLUGIN 02 / IMPECCABLE` chip (white tracked caps in a solid
   black bar with a torn/offset right edge) is a strong recurring device; the YAML has only the outlined counter chip.
7. **No type scale.** Four type roles are named (woodtype display, ultra-heavy geometric lowercase, its lighter weight,
   marker). No sizes, no ratios, no minimum body size. In the sources the display runs 6-8x the body.
8. **Texture is named but not budgeted.** "distressed ink texture", "misregistration", "grain" — no amount. Too much
   and it reads as a filter; the sources are subtle (grain visible, misregistration only at solid edges).
9. **No `list_mode`.** `text_density: high` + a middle band explicitly reserved for a paragraph, and the letterpress
   grammar (numbered rows under a rule) is a natural fit.
10. **No margin numbers.** "wide paper margins on every side" is the whole look of a letterpress poster and it is the
    one thing left unmeasured.

### Budget realism — **PASS for the paragraph, FAIL for the headline**
`headline 110 / subline 160 / slide 300`. The middle-band paragraph genuinely holds 300 characters at reading size
(source 04's body is ~150 and has room for double). The headline is the problem: "towering… filling two thirds of the
frame" at 90 effective characters is impossible — source 05's headline is 38 characters and already fills the frame.
The style must either declare a step-down ladder for long headlines or accept ~55.

### SUGGESTED FIELD TEXT

```yaml
    typography: >-
      Cover display: condensed woodtype-style uppercase, set tight (tracking -2%), leading 0.88 of cap height, cap
      height 14-20% of frame height — and it steps DOWN one tier per extra line rather than shrinking the line count.
      Body-slide headline: ultra-heavy geometric lowercase sans, cap height 8-11% of frame height, two lines, one
      phrase reversed inside a solid black bar. Paragraph: the lighter weight of that same geometric sans, cap height
      3-4% of frame height, leading 1.35, line length 30-46 characters, one phrase per paragraph allowed in the heavy
      weight. Chips, counters and meta lines: letterspaced mono or tracked caps at 1.6-2% of frame height. Hand-drawn
      marker for annotations only. Every letterform carries ink texture and slightly ragged edges — never a clean
      vector outline. Display : paragraph size ratio stays between 5:1 and 8:1 on every slide.
    palette:
      - "COVER GROUND — aged cream paper #E8DCC3 with visible tooth; the cover and only the cover"
      - "BODY GROUND — terracotta #B5573C with film grain; every body slide and never the cover"
      - "INK 1 (on cream) — black #1A1714: headline setup lines, paragraph, frame rules"
      - "INK 2 (on cream) — vermilion #E2522B: the payoff line, the kicker, the counter chip outline, drawn marks"
      - "INK 1 (on terracotta) — cream #F3E9D8: headline and paragraph"
      - "INK 2 (on terracotta) — black #1A1714: the reversed highlight bar, the label chip, drawn marks"
      - "Exactly one paper tone and exactly two inks per slide — the pair is fixed by which ground the slide uses"
    text_placement: >-
      Paper margins of 8% left and right, 6% top, 8% bottom; nothing crosses them. Cover: headline occupies the top
      two thirds flush left; the deck line sits under a hand-drawn underline in the lower-left quadrant; the drawn cue
      sits lower-right; the outlined counter chip top-left. Body slide: headline in the upper-left third, paragraph
      filling the middle band left-aligned and ragged right, inset card in the lower half, counter chip top-left and
      the letterspaced mono meta line beside it. Everything is flush left to one margin — the style has exactly one
      alignment axis and never centres anything.
    image_treatment: >-
      Flat printed inks on textured paper: paper tooth visible at 100% of the ground, ink density variation inside
      solids, and plate misregistration of no more than one or two pixels showing ONLY at the edges of large solids —
      never on body-size type, which stays readable. A pixel-art mascot is the only illustration and appears at most
      once per slide. Screenshot insets ride on a white card held by two translucent tape strips at opposite corners,
      rotated no more than three degrees, never bleeding to the frame edge. No photograph of a place, no gradient, no
      gloss, no soft drop shadow, no 3D render.
    visual_pacing: >-
      One enormous typographic statement per slide, one supporting paragraph, one drawn mark. The cover is cream, every
      body slide is terracotta, and a deck never mixes them or introduces a third ground. Margin, chip form, ink pair
      per ground, rule weight and mascot treatment are identical across the deck; only the words, the inset and the
      drawn mark's position change.
    list_mode:
      reflow_over_chars: 200
      max_rows: 7
      layout: >-
        A list panel sets as numbered letterpress rows in the middle band: the number in the second ink at the
        paragraph's heavy weight, hanging in the left margin of the text block, and the row's text beside it in the
        lighter weight. One source line per row, always; a number and its text never separate; rows share one left
        edge, one baseline rhythm and one size, and all shrink together rather than one being cut. An over-long row
        wraps under its own first word, clear of the hanging number. A hairline rule in the second ink separates rows
        only when there are five or more.
      overflow: reflow
```

## 4. `meme-caricature-panels`

**Source:** `SuperFunny_Polarizing_MemeStyle_Caricature_Promotional_01.png` — two framed panels of equal height on a
cream ground, each preceded by a full-width cream caption band carrying one heavy condensed black sentence with a
terminal period; top panel = sepia clutter, exhausted figure from behind; bottom panel = warm lamp, violet-blue robot
reclining; `HYPEDIGITALY` letterspaced grey in the bottom margin.

This is the **best-matched** style in the registry — the YAML and the source agree almost everywhere. The gaps are
about precision, not direction.

### Gaps
1. **Panel geometry unspecified.** "Two stacked comic panels of equal height with thin black frames" — no frame
   weight, no gutter, no panel aspect, no outer border. The source has a distinct 1-2px black frame, a cream gutter of
   about 4% of frame height, and a visible outer border around the whole composition.
2. **Caption band vs "paper margin" is imprecise.** The YAML says captions live "in the paper margin above each
   panel". The source is stronger than that: a **full-width cream band** of fixed height sits above each panel, and the
   caption is vertically centred in it. That fixed band is what keeps the two beats parallel.
3. **No type scale.** "heavy condensed sans" with no size. Source caption cap height ≈ 4.5% of frame height.
4. **Caption alignment mismatch (minor).** Zone says "centred"; the source is centred — but the second caption is
   noticeably wider than the first and both stay on one line. Say that both captions set at the SAME size regardless of

## 9. Cross-style summary — systemic gaps

Ordered by my estimate of impact on the 2/12 publishable rate.

### S1. No type scale anywhere (8/8 styles)
Every `typography` field names families, weights and casing. **Not one names a size, a ratio, or a relationship.**
"Very large", "oversized", "poster scale", "towering", "at reading size" — the model re-decides all of these on every
slide, which is exactly what makes a deck look like eight unrelated posters. **Fix: every style states cap height as a
percentage of frame height for each type role, plus a fixed ratio ladder between roles, plus a minimum size floor.**
This is the single highest-value edit in T1.6 and it costs one sentence per style.

### S2. No step-down ladder for long verbatim strings (8/8)
Since D46/FR-304 the on-image text is a **verbatim quote of arbitrary length that may never be trimmed**, yet every
style describes its headline as a single fixed size ("two to three lines at very large size"). When a 90-character
quote arrives there is no declared behaviour, so the model improvises: it shrinks past legibility, it overflows, or the
copy stage marks the panel unofferable and ships a wordless slide. **Fix: every style with a headline zone declares an
explicit characters → line-count → size ladder** (as drafted in §5, §7, §8 above). This is the design-side counterpart
of decision 5 ("verbatim always wins").

### S3. Palettes are colour lists, not colour systems (8/8)
Every `palette` is five poetic names + hexes with **no roles, no coverage limits, and no statement of what may never be
tinted**. The model has no way to know which hex is ground and which is accent, so accents flood and grounds drift.
**Fix: role-prefixed palette strings** (`"GROUND — …"`, `"ACCENT — … never more than a tenth of the frame"`) as drafted
throughout. The field is a free-string list, so this needs no parser change.

### S4. No numbers in any spatial rule (8/8)
"Generous margins", "wide paper margins", "clear of every edge", "upper third". Nothing is measurable, so nothing is
repeatable, and nothing protects against a 4:5 crop eating a caption. **Fix: percent-of-frame margins, a stated
alignment axis, a stated vertical rhythm, and a bottom safe-area rule in every `text_placement`.**

### S5. No list or data treatment existed at all before this wave (8/8)
Not one style says how a numbered list, a label/value pair, a comparison or a table should look — while `text_density:
high` styles are routinely assigned exactly that content, and FR-329 (pair integrity) is about to start *judging* it.
**`list_mode` is needed on 6 of 8 styles:** `editorial-voxel-carousel`, `letterpress-print-carousel`,
`platform-showcase-card`, `hypelead-brand-card` (all four are `text_density: high` and list-shaped by construction),
plus `anime-noir-statement` (needs a *sanctioned* one — see S9) and `photoreal-ambient-caption` /
`ugc-tabletop-statement` (need modest ones so a list panel doesn't become a prose blob).
**`meme-caricature-panels` should deliberately NOT get one** — and should say so in a comment.

### S6. Deck-level consistency is asserted, not specified (7/8)
Only `anime-noir-statement` says clearly what is pinned across a deck and what may vary (its "THIS SCENE OUTRANKS THE
SLIDE'S VISUAL BRIEF" paragraph — **use it as the model for the other seven**). Everywhere else, `visual_pacing` is one
poetic line and `carousel_slide` covers cover-vs-body only. Under anchor chaining, drift between slides 2..N is a
craft/system-critic failure waiting to happen. **Fix: every `visual_pacing` ends with an explicit FIXED-list and a
MAY-CHANGE list.**

### S7. Motif inventories with no budget (5/8)
`hypelead-brand-card` (ten devices), `editorial-voxel-carousel` (sparkles, ticks, script, rule, chips, terminal card),
`letterpress-print-carousel` (mascot, tape, underlines, circles), `platform-showcase-card`,
`photoreal-ambient-caption`. Named without counts, they appear in random subsets on every slide. **Fix: per-frame
counts ("four to eight sparkles", "at most three cards, never a fourth").**

### S8. The greeking rule is broken in the same way in four styles
`platform-showcase-card`, `editorial-voxel-carousel`, `letterpress-print-carousel` and `hypelead-brand-card` all state
greeking as an **exclusion** with an "unless quoted" escape buried inside it. A render model reads the exclusion and
greeks everything — including planned verbatim text — and the source material shows the rule is also *too broad*
(the sources' mockups keep headings, nav and hero imagery legible; only body copy is greeked).
**Fix, uniform across all four:** state greeking positively in `image_treatment` as *"greeked bars are background
mock-up filler and carry no meaning; a string the TEXT block quotes for the mock-up renders literally, in full, in its
own language, and is never greeked, shortened or replaced by a bar"*, and reduce the exclusion to a pointer. This also
gives the brief critic a clean `sanctioned_illegible` derivation.

### S9. Budget realism: two styles will silently produce wordless slides
- **`meme-caricature-panels`: `slide: 60`** — most real source panels exceed 60 characters, so most mapped slides ship
  wordless. Raise to ~110 with a two-line caption band, or keep this style off mapped decks.
- **`ugc-tabletop-statement`: `slide: 120`** — contradicts its own `carousel_slide` promise. Raise to ~200.
- **`anime-noir-statement`: `slide: 180` with no body zone** — structurally unrenderable until the sanctioned list
  treatment (§5) or a body zone exists.
- Conversely, `headline: 110` in four styles is **dead** (config caps at 90); harmless but misleading — and it hides
  the real problem, which is that 90 does not fit any of those headline zones at their declared scale (S2).

### S10. Contradictions between a style and its own Inspiration source (5/8)
`photoreal-ambient-caption` (daylight-only vs two night sources; regular weight vs bold; upper-left vs centred),
`editorial-voxel-carousel` (teal vs orange/green; lettered swipe; legible terminal),
`letterpress-print-carousel` (lettered swipe; legible inset; "two inks" vs three),
`platform-showcase-card` (centred vs flush left; "wireframe" vs designed screen; "no photography" vs a photographic
hero inside the card). Each needs a **resolution, not a widening** — per the header's own M9 rule, an unresolved
either/or is worse than either branch. Where the ban is deliberate (faces, lettered swipe words, real account names),
**say it is deliberate in a comment** so a later author doesn't "fix" it back into a leakage defect.

### S11. Two structural odds and ends
- **No reference material exists for `hypelead-brand-card`** — the registry's most complex style is entirely
  unreferenced, and its palette is uncorroborated in the repo. Operator input needed.
- **Reel-capable styles carry no motion spec.** `photoreal-ambient-caption`, `anime-noir-statement` and
  `ugc-tabletop-statement` declare `format_affinity: [… reel]` and a `motion_profile`, but nothing says what moves.
  One sentence in `image_treatment` each ("in motion only X and Y move; the camera is locked") closes it.

### S12. Parser reminder for T1.6 (repeat of §0.1)
Unknown top-level style keys are **silently dropped**, not rejected. Any new spec invented outside the existing field
set — a `type_scale:` block, a `grid:` block, a `motifs:` block — would parse cleanly, validate cleanly, and reach the
render model as absolutely nothing. Everything above is deliberately written to fit inside `typography`, `palette`,
`text_placement`, `image_treatment`, `visual_pacing`, `layout_zones[].text_treatment`, `per_format_guidance` and the
one sanctioned new field, `list_mode`.
