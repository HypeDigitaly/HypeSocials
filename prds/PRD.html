TL;DR — Plain English

HypeSocials creates viral social media posts directly from trending topics. You pick how many images, slide-decks and short videos you want, set your budget, and press Enter. About three minutes later — eight to ten if you asked for videos — you have a folder of finished posts ready to publish.

What happens in between: Virlo provides trending topics as text (captions, hooks, panel texts, metadata). The engine screens for brand safety, picks the strongest topics, quotes exact words from the trending posts' on-image text and captions, applies one of nineteen defined visual styles — chosen by deterministic rotation or optional LLM-matched fit assessment (you control these via a YAML registry — colors, typography, layout, match profiles), optionally adds your wordmark in a unified teal palette, and generates images, slide-decks or videos. For slideshows, each source slide's text and visuals are analyzed and mapped to the output slides. Style choices and which posts were quoted are all visible in the gallery so nothing is hidden.

Every finished picture is now checked before it ships. Three separate AI critics look at each rendered frame — one checks the words are the right words and nobody else's name or logo sneaked in, one checks it looks like the style it was supposed to be, one checks it is simply well made. Anything they fail gets re-made with a targeted fix, up to three tries. A creative that still cannot pass is blocked: you keep the files and a plain-English report of what was wrong, but it is never treated as ready to publish. The critics only ever see the finished picture — never the instructions used to make it — so they cannot be talked into approving something.

Key facts: Cost is estimated and confirmed before anything is billed. If the estimate is over budget and you're sitting there, it refuses and offers smaller numbers; if run unattended, it drops posts from the end until it fits. Every step is logged — if one post fails, the rest ship. You can stop at any time (Ctrl+C). You can preview trends and their verdicts for free. The quality gate never spends money you did not approve: its re-render budget is shown up front and it stops rather than overspending.

Pipeline Flow

Caveats: "Slide Intelligence" runs after the Confirm gate (paid LLM) but before render submission; costs ~$0.01–0.03 per post if sourced from slideshows (charged to budget). A reel enters the gate at its seed frame only — the finished clip is never judged, because frame extraction would mean ffmpeg (NFR-25). Re-renders are targeted: only failing frames are re-made, and only with canned remedy sentences — a critic's own prose never reaches a render payload. The gallery includes source slides with extracted text/visuals aligned to our output slides for full provenance.

Why: The Pivot

Three core inversions from the pre-pivot design:

  • Visual authority: Was vision-analyzed Virlo images → Now a local, versioned styles.yaml registry (19 textual style definitions as of v2.4.0: palette, typography, layout, stored in the repo, edited like code). No style reference images; rendering is driven by text-only metadata. For slideshows, source slides are analyzed post-Confirm (Sonnet 5) to extract on-image text and visual descriptions enriching render prompts. This makes fidelity auditable and removes 262 lines of vision-analysis code.
  • Copy: Was LLM-generated "our words" → Now verbatim quotes from trending posts' on-image text (panel texts, hooks, overlays) and captions, selected by an AI but resolved to bytes by the engine. Descriptions never render. Every rendered word is from a real post in its source language. Eliminates hallucination and removes 210 lines of copywrite code.
  • Topics: Was monitor-based grouping → Now topics extracted per monitor (up to 9 each) with per-topic strength computed from their own posts' views. Unlocks post-level recency: fresh posts from the same monitor within the history window, breaking the old 2-reuse bottleneck (measured 30–39 sets now available).

The Gauntlet Gate — FR-322–330 (v2.2.0)

Two of twelve decks in the 2026-08-14 carousel audit were publishable. The Gauntlet is the answer: the engine stops being a one-shot renderer and starts reviewing its own output before the operator ever sees it. It replaces the old one-shot vision check (FR-105) outright.

Three critics, one job each

CriticJudgesDefect codes
brief Contract fidelity and leakage — presence, never quality missing_text, invented_text, translated, pair_break, missing_mark, forbidden_mark, platform_chrome, identity_leak, counter_value, signature
system Style contract and cross-frame consistency style_palette, style_layout, style_consistency, counter_placement
craft Execution quality — execution, never content garbled, truncated, contrast, logo_fidelity, composition, frame_integrity

The enums are enforced in-schema, which is what mechanically stops craft from ruling on words and brief from ruling on beauty. Each critic sees the rendered frames and the contract data — never the assembled render prompt, never the reference set, never another critic's verdict.

Three tiers of failure

TierStanding after the final roundOutcome
leakageidentity_leak, forbidden_mark, platform_chrome, invented_text, translatedAlways BLOCKED, regardless of config — someone else's name or mark on a published frame is the most expensive error this pipeline can make
contractOther brief codes and all system codesPer fail_action — block (default) or degrade (ships with a tag)
craftAll craft codesNever blocks — ships with a GAUNTLET_CRAFT tag (knob: craft_blocks)

What keeps it honest

  • Verbatim always wins. The loop may never shorten, re-word, translate or drop a quoted string. A style's list_mode is a reflow trigger, never a ceiling — a long panel gets set differently, not cut (D50).
  • The fix channel is sealed. Re-render instructions are assembled from canned remedy sentences keyed by (defect code, zone), capped at 600 characters, stripped of competitor and creator terms, and closed with a fence line stating the fix section contains no words to render. A critic's free text goes to the report and the console — never into a render payload.
  • A broken gate never costs a deck. An unparseable critic is retried once, then dropped for the deck with a degraded_gate tag. If every critic is unavailable the result is skipped — the creative ships as-is, tagged, and is never BLOCKED.
  • Spend is bounded and never gating. Re-renders are discretionary against a per-deck budget (default $0.30). The pre-Confirm estimate displays the worst-case gauntlet allowance but never adds it to the expected cost and never trims a creative to make room (FR-106a).
  • Money and time are the caller's business. The gauntlet module neither prices nor spends: budget, runway and reference assembly live in the re-render closure that owns them.

Honest caveat, stated in FR-322: the critics do receive the style DNA and layout zones — the same words the render prompt uses. That is the only possible referent for judging style adherence, so it is disclosed rather than hidden.

Design Decisions — The Pivot, the Gate, and Style Intelligence (D41–D57)

D1–D22, D25–D30 are in the detailed PRD files. D41–D53 define the post-pivot architecture, slideshow fidelity, and the v2.2.0 quality gate. D54–D57 (v2.3.0–v2.4.0) introduce compress mode for carousel copy and the v2.4.0 registry expansion with LLM-matched style assignment and teal-spine unification:

D41

Meta-style Registry (Text-Only)

Visual authority: nineteen textual style definitions in styles.yaml (9 originals + build-log-mono + 4 archetype + 5 teal variants; colors, typography, layout, motion profile, match profiles for LLM). No reference images attached to styles; rendering driven by metadata only. No vision analysis, no built-in fallback tier. Missing registry → exit 2. Deterministic rotation or optional LLM-matched assignment per creative.

D42

Verbatim Copy via Selection

Luna selects exact words from trending posts: on-image panel texts and hooks first, then captions (never descriptions). Selections fit the style's budget and are free of @handles, URLs, emoji. Engine resolves to bytes verbatim, never re-typed. Source language kept, no translation. Degrade: caption-only or top-post fallback.

D43

Branding: Selector + Two Profiles

Config-driven: branding.brand picks HypeDigitaly or HypeLead (never mixed); brand_ratio deterministically applies wordmark to a fraction; wordmark rendered as text via the image model, never composite. Brand-slot styles (e.g., brand-card) collapse branding block.

D44

Monitor → Topics Architecture

Per-monitor data split into up to 9 topics. Each topic has a view-ranked SourcePost list. Per-topic strength computed from each topic's own posts. History key changes from <monitor_id> to <mid>::<topic_key>. Enables post-level recency (30–39 sets per monitor vs. old 2).

D45

Console Observability Mandate

Step-by-step visibility: stage narration with in→out counts (FR-296), topics table showing sort proof and per-post views (FR-297), per-creative provenance with copy source (FR-298), render heartbeats and verbosity tiers (FR-299), menu re-shape with runnability facts (FR-300).

D46

Slideshow Fidelity (v2.1.0)

Fixes from paid-run recon: fetch 30-day recent window ranked by views within pool (not all-time); quote on-image panel texts and hooks first, captions for captions, never descriptions; panel-mapped carousels (source slide N → output slide N verbatim); post-level never-repeat protection; slide intelligence analysis post-Confirm to extract text and visuals; provenance gallery showing source slides with extracted data aligned to our renders.

D49

Gauntlet Gate Supersedes the No-Loop Doctrine

D3's "zero gates, renders once and ships" and NFR-4's blanket one-attempt rule are withdrawn for this one sanctioned case. Blind retries stay capped at one; the Gauntlet's bounded, critic-directed rounds (≤ rounds_max, budget- and runway-gated) are the exception. FR-105's machinery and vision_check_question.md are retired, not kept as a fallback lane — gauntlet.enabled: false means no post-render gate at all.

D50

Verbatim-Wins List Mode

A style may declare list_mode — a reflow trigger with reflow_over_chars, max_rows, layout prose and an overflow strategy. It is consumed only as layout guidance in the render prompt. It is never a character ceiling, never enters the budget line, and is never consulted by the panel-drop verdict. No text is ever dropped for a style reason.

D51

Spend Runway + Deck Viability

No discretionary or projected render submits when the remaining run time is under the job's timeout plus grace — an unbilled refusal with cause NO_RUNWAY that never consumes FR-317's one resubmit. Pre-committed work is exempt, so bookkeeping can never split a deck. A slide permanently lost to a render defect makes its deck unsalvageable: no further work is submitted for it, though already-paid jobs are never cancelled.

D52

Seeded Style Rotation

Rotation was a pure function of entry.order, so every run opened with the same style in the same slot. The pick becomes pool[(order + step + run_seed) % n] with run_seed a stable hash of the run id — still deterministic within a run and reproducible from its id, but no longer identical across runs. Knob: styles.rotation: seeded | fixed.

D53

Pillow Scope Extended to Crop Validation

D48 admitted Pillow for one job: cropping sanctioned tool-logo patches. D53 extends it to validating those crops — a pixel-variance floor that refuses near-uniform patches, and content-rect detection used only as a fallback when the first crop fails validation. Still no compositing, no resizing pipelines, no thumbnails.

D54

Compress Mode for Carousel Decks (v2.3.0)

Operator-opted copy compression: in compress mode (versus verbatim default), the copy LLM receives the bound post's panel texts verbatim and returns compressed text fitting the style's budget, humanized (no comment bait, hashtags checked), in source language, never invented. One-walk invariant guarantees gauntlet contract integrity. Fallback: failed compress call yields verbatim mapped deck tagged copy_degraded. Engine default is verbatim. D58 (v2.4.1, 2026-08-20) withdrew the shipped compress pin — all three brand configs now ship verbatim — an operator preference for the source’s own words, with the density cost accepted rather than removed. Measured on run 20260820_145809_4a0q: the dense D56 archetypes share the registry’s 300-char slide ceiling with 9 of 19 styles and FR-304(a) forbids a budget from dropping mapped text, so verbatim shipped 500–1,180 character panels onto 300-char frames (8 of 9 decks over budget, 6 of 9 delivered). The compress mechanism is untouched and still reachable per run via --copy-mode compress.

D55

Style Doubling-Down (v2.3.0)

Shipped brand configs narrow the rotation pool to four handpicked styles: anime-noir-statement, letterpress-print-carousel, meme-caricature-panels, and the new quiet-luxury-night-photoreal. meme-caricature-panels is carousel_role: slides_only and stays inert on carousel-only runs (kept deliberately), so effective carousel rotation is 3 styles. Standing decision D-G ("colour is curated by choosing styles, never by editing a style") is preserved. Pipeline & Walkthrough

A step-by-step journey through a run:

  1. Launch: Menu/CLI, pick config, format mix, budget, quality gate (--gauntlet / --no-gauntlet).
  2. Collect: Virlo MCP fetches trending topics (text, captions, metadata only; no image downloads).
  3. Split, Filter, Select: Topics extracted from monitors; competitor filter screens for brand safety; strongest topics ranked and selected for requested creatives.
  4. Assign: Deterministic style rotation assigns affine styles (format + brand compatible) per creative. Deterministic brand rotation applies wordmark to brand_ratio fraction.
  5. Quote: Luna selects exact words from the topic's top posts (on-image text, captions) that fit the style's character budget and pass filters. Engine resolves to bytes verbatim. Never descriptions.
  6. Analyze (if slideshows): Claude Sonnet 5 reads each source slideshow, extracting on-image text verbatim and visual descriptions (charts, icons, composition) for each slide. Slides stored to gallery folder for provenance.
  7. Generate (Wave 1): Images, carousel slide 1 (anchor), reel seed frames submitted concurrently. Text-only style definitions drive rendering (no style reference images).
  8. Generate (Wave 2): Carousel slides 2–N mapped to source panels verbatim, reel animation (seed frame only, no motion reference) submitted after Wave 1 completes or pre-committed.
  9. Gauntlet (post-render gate): Three fresh-context Sonnet 5 critics judge the rendered frames against contract data. Failing frames re-render with canned targeted fixes, up to 3 rounds per deck (1 for standalone images and reel seed frames). The carousel anchor is gated first, before slides 2–N are submitted.
  10. Terminal verdict: All critics pass ⇒ ship. Leakage standing ⇒ BLOCKED always. Other contract defects ⇒ per fail_action. Craft-only ⇒ ships tagged. A BLOCKED creative keeps its artifacts, writes GAUNTLET_REPORT.yaml and BLOCKED.txt, records no trend-history use, and forces exit 1.
  11. Package: Per-asset folders, gallery.html with source slides and extracted data, run log, trend history updated (post-level never-repeat).

Preview Modes (No Generation)

  • --preview-sources: Show all topics and their verdicts (keep/strip/skip). Zero LLM/Kie spend. Virlo API cost only.
  • --preview-analysis: Also run Luna copy. Show topics, verdicts, assigned styles, verbatim copy selections. LLM cost; Kie cost zero.
